Hotel review argument mining task. Extract body literals from the text span.

A body literal is a short phrase in snake_case that names ONE quality the text expresses about the topic.

Topic     : {{TOPIC}}
Sentiment : {{SENTIMENT}}
Text span : "{{SELECTED_CONTENT}}"

How to form a body literal:
  - Find each distinct quality or attribute mentioned about {{TOPIC}}
  - Write it as: quality_words_topic_noun  (e.g. clean_room, helpful_staff, close_to_airport)
  - Only use words from the original text — never add new words
  - Remove articles (a, an, the) and degree adverbs (very, extremely, quite, so) — keep everything else
  - Use present tense, lowercase, underscores instead of spaces
  - Keep compound phrases whole (e.g. "easy stroll to nice restaurant" → easy_stroll_to_nice_restaurant)

Reference examples:
Topic=Staff | "Staff is extremely helpful and easy to communicate with"
  → {"body_literals": ["helpful_staff", "easy_to_communicate_with"]}

Topic=Food | "Tasty food on the first floor, comfortable restaurant"

IMPORTANT: The SENTIMENT tells you which side to extract. A text may contain both good and bad
qualities — extract ONLY those that match the given sentiment.

Output ONLY a single JSON object, no explanation:
