Understand the dataset.
Our tasks are to tag 4 parts: 1) Topic, 2) Sentiment, 3) Head, 4) Body.
Body is the most difficult (lots of rules) and needs more attention.
The ABA labeling consists of 3 parts 1) Head, 2) Body, 3) Contrary.
You don't need to tag Contrary. It will be automatically tagged based on Body.
Example of Positive body: Body is comfortable_bed
Contrary is no_evident_not_comfortable_bed
Example of Negative body: Body is no_soundproof
Contrary is have_evident_no_soundproof
Checkpoint:
1. You will see that the meaning of body and contrary are the same
2. Read the whole sentence by combining head and body, check if it makes sense.
3. Read the whole sentence by combining head and contrary, check if it makes sense.
Both sentences (2, 3) must be understandable otherwise we need to fix the body or unlabel
it (tag “Off”).
